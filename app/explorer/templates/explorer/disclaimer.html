Before you start

This app is a research demonstrator of the toxage-sim project. Please read the limitations and the terms of use.

Limitations

  • All patients are synthetic. They were generated by a computer program from published summary figures and from assumptions of the project. No record belongs to a real person.
  • The risk models are simple first versions. Several of their parameters are assumptions, not estimates from patient data. They have not been validated on real patients.
  • The app is not a medical device. It must not be used for diagnosis, treatment decisions or any other clinical purpose. Nothing shown here is medical advice.
  • The data have no follow-up over time yet. Each patient has one record at the start of a therapy line and the outcomes that followed.
