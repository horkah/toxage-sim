What this is

Cancer therapies save and prolong lives, but they often harm the patient as well. In clinical trials of systemic treatments for advanced cancer, more than half of the patients have at least one severe side effect, and some of them are noticed late. The project asks how well toxicity can be predicted at the start of a therapy, and how much earlier it could be detected.

It works with synthetic data only. A data engine simulates patients with advanced pancreatic, prostate or breast cancer week by week, based on published trials and guidelines. Risk models estimate the risk of severe side effects at therapy start. Intervention scenarios show what risk-adapted dosing and monitoring could change. This app brings the three together.
