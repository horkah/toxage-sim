What this is

Cancer therapies save and prolong lives, but they often harm the patient as well. In clinical trials of systemic treatments for advanced cancer, more than half of the patients have at least one severe side effect, and some of them are noticed late. The project asks how well toxicity can be predicted at the start of a therapy, and how much earlier it could be detected.

It works with synthetic data only. A data engine simulates patients with advanced pancreatic, prostate or breast cancer week by week, based on published trials and guidelines. Risk models estimate the risk of severe side effects at therapy start. Intervention scenarios show what risk-adapted dosing and monitoring could change. This app brings the three together.

New here? “Help” on top starts a guided tour through every part of the app.

Simple view

This installation opens in a simple view, made to show the essence in about ten minutes. The explorer has three views: the risk of severe side effects, the course of a single patient, and what the interventions change. Each shows the charts that tell the most, with a few filters. A data set is made from the number of patients alone, under every scenario.

Data, models and calculations are the same as in the full view; only less is shown and offered. Settings › View › Advanced shows everything: all charts and filters, the full record of a patient, the choice of scenarios, tumour types and regimens when generating, archives, and the model parameters. The guided tours show what the view in use offers.

Data protection

Server and network

The app runs on a server protected by a firewall and uses no outside services. Everything your browser loads, including fonts, scripts and charts, comes from this server. There are no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Your information

The app never asks for personal information: there are no user accounts and no fields for names, addresses or health data, and all patients shown are synthetic. It keeps only what it needs to work: cookies for the accepted terms, the chosen data set, a running demo and, in a demo installation, your own space, and your settings such as theme and text size in this browser. Apart from the connection data that every web server needs to deliver a page, the app processes no personal data.
