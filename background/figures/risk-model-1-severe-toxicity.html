Calculation
logit p = logit rR + ∑k ln(ORk) xk
  • rR: published rate of severe toxicity with regimen R, the risk of the reference patient
  • xk: (age − 65)/10, 1[ECOG ≥ 2], number of impaired domains, 12.3 − Hb, (38 − albumin)/10, 1[reduced dose]
Example
Trastuzumab deruxtecan (53%); 75 years, ECOG 2, two impaired domains, Hb 11.3 g/dl, albumin 33 g/l, full dose
OR = 1.10 · 2.00 · 1.21 · 1.11 · 1.16 = 3.43
odds = 0.526/0.474 · 3.43 = 3.81
p = odds/(1 + odds) = 79%
