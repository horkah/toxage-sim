Calculation
logit p = logit r0,R + ∑k ln(ORk) xk
r0,R such that Eref[p] = rR
  • rR: published rate of the event with regimen R
  • r0,R: risk of the reference patient; the mean over a reference population with the published shares of the factors equals rR
  • xk: yes-or-no factors, eGFR − 80, white cells − 7
Example
ADT + docetaxel; 70 years, no earlier chemotherapy, eGFR 60 ml/min, white cells 6.0, no G-CSF
OR = 1.30 · 1.15 · 1.08 = 1.60
odds = 0.527/0.473 · 1.60 = 1.79
p = odds/(1 + odds) = 64%
The same patient with G-CSF from the first cycle: 18%.
