Calculation
p = 1 − (1 − r0,A)HR
HR = HRprior,Ae · 1.4(age − 71)/10 · ∏k HRkxk
rA = 1 − (1 − 0.615 · qA)364/336
  • A: type of androgen deprivation; e: earlier myocardial infarction or stroke
  • qA: rate in HERO within 48 weeks, death from any cause included; 0.615: cardiovascular share of these events in PRONOUNCE
  • r0,A: risk of a man of 71 without factors; the mean over a reference population equals rA
Example
GnRH agonist; 75 years, earlier myocardial infarction, diabetes
HR = 1.46 · 1.14 · 1.50 = 2.51
p = 1 − (1 − 0.023)2.51 = 5.7%
The same man on a GnRH antagonist: 3.2%.
