Calculation
logit p = logit r0,R + ∑k ln(ORk) xk
r0,R such that Eref[p] = rR
  • rR: published rate of the event with regimen R
  • r0,R: risk without any factor; the mean over a reference population equals rR
  • UGT1A1*28 counts with NALIRIFOX and sacituzumab govitecan, which both act through SN-38
Example
NALIRIFOX; 72 years, ECOG 1, albumin 33 g/l, two copies of UGT1A1*28
OR = 1.30 · 1.30 · 2.00 = 3.38
odds = 0.167/0.833 · 3.38 = 0.68
p = odds/(1 + odds) = 40%
The same patient without the genotype: 25%.
