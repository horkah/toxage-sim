Calculation
p = 1 − (1 − r0)HR,   HR = ∏k HRkxk
p(t) = p · F(t) / F(730)
F(t) = Φ((ln t − ln 129) / 0.92)
  • r0: risk without any factor, set so that the mean risk in the pooled population of the published model equals the rate of the regimen
  • Onset log-normal with a median of 129 days; grade 2 or higher: 0.711 · p
  • Remaining risk, free of the event on day t: (p − p(t)) / (1 − p(t))
Example
Trastuzumab deruxtecan; 58 years, renal function mildly decreased, SpO2 93%, diagnosis 2 years ago
HR = 1.56 · 1.24 · 2.14 = 4.14
